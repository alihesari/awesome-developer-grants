# Awesome Developer Grants [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of money for developers and open-source maintainers: grants, funds, cloud and compute credits, research awards, government programs and sponsorship platforms.

Every entry links to the program's official page. Terms, amounts and deadlines change often, so confirm on the linked page before you apply.

## Open Source Grants and Funds

- [Alpha-Omega](https://alpha-omega.dev/) - Security fund backed by large technology companies that invests in critical open source projects. Offer: an annual budget of over $7M directed at selected projects, with no open application form published.
- [Ethereum Foundation ESP](https://esp.ethereum.foundation/applicants) - Rolling support for free and open-source work that strengthens the Ethereum ecosystem. Offer: no fixed range published, amounts set by scope; open to individuals and companies as long as the funded work is open source.
- [FUTO Grants](https://futo.tech/grants/) - Funding for independent software that keeps computing under user control. Offer: $1,000 to $5,000 microgrants for small or early-stage projects, plus larger Legendary Grants for established projects; applications are open.
- [Gitcoin](https://www.gitcoin.co/) - Quadratic funding rounds that match community donations to open source and public goods work. Offer: no fixed amount published, awards depend on the matching pool of a live round.
- [GitHub Secure Open Source Fund](https://github.com/open-source/github-secure-open-source-fund) - Three-week program that pays maintainers to harden the security of their projects. Offer: $10,000 per project in three tranches; maintainers aged 18 or over in GitHub Sponsors regions who lead a licensed, adopted project, rolling applications.
- [NLnet Foundation](https://nlnet.nl/propose/) - Open calls for technical work on free software, open hardware and open standards. Offer: between €5,000 and €50,000 for a first grant; projects must be technical and released under open licences.
- [NumFOCUS Small Development Grants](https://numfocus.org/programs/small-development-grants) - Small grants for targeted development work on scientific computing projects. Offer: up to $10,000 per proposal; only NumFOCUS fiscally sponsored or affiliated projects, one proposal per cycle.
- [OTF Internet Freedom Fund](https://www.opentech.fund/funds/internet-freedom-fund/) - Supports open technology and research that counters censorship and surveillance. Offer: $10,000 to $900,000 for up to 24 months, rolling applications open to individuals and organizations of any nationality.
- [OTF Rapid Response Fund](https://www.opentech.fund/funds/rapid-response-fund/) - Emergency support for people and groups facing an active digital attack. Offer: $1 to $50,000 for a period of six months or less; activists, journalists, human rights defenders and other high-risk users.
- [Python Software Foundation Grants](https://www.python.org/psf/grants/) - Funds Python conferences and workshops run by community organizers. Offer: up to $2,000 for a conference and up to $1,500 for a workshop, from a set annual budget; events need at least 60% Python content.
- [Sentry for Open Source](https://sentry.io/for/open-source/) - Sponsored error monitoring, tracing and session replay for open source projects. Offer: 5M errors, 1B spans, 100K replays and business features with no term limit; projects under a friendly licence such as Apache or MIT.
- [Solana Foundation Grants](https://solana.org/grants) - Funds public-goods and open source work in the Solana ecosystem. Offer: no amount published; open to individuals, teams, companies, nonprofits, governments and academics, reviewed on a rolling basis.
- [Sovereign Tech Fund](https://www.sovereign.tech/programs/fund) - Public money for the open digital base technologies that other software depends on. Offer: the work described must cost more than €50,000; libraries, package managers, protocol implementations and administration tools under OSI or FSF licences.
- [Stellar Community Fund](https://communityfund.stellar.org/) - Awards for developers building products on the Stellar network. Offer: up to $150,000 in XLM through the Build track, plus smaller Instawards for early-stage builders.
- [Zcash Community Grants](https://zcashcommunitygrants.org/) - Funds independent teams doing ongoing work for the Zcash ecosystem. Offer: drawn from a pool of roughly 100,000 ZEC available through November 2028; open applications for wallets, infrastructure, applications and education.

## Cloud and Startup Credits

- [Atlassian for Startups](https://www.atlassian.com/software/startups) - Free premium tiers of Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket and Loom for one year. Offer: 12 months free for up to 50 users; not an existing paid customer, venture funded or accelerator affiliated, and no more than $10 million raised.
- [Auth0 for Startups](https://auth0.com/startups) - Hosted authentication and identity for early-stage products. Offer: one year of B2B Professional with up to 100,000 monthly active users; venture-backed with under $5M raised, under $1M ARR and under two years since incorporation.
- [AWS Activate](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits) - Credits for Amazon Web Services across its full product range. Offer: up to $5,000 for self-funded founders and up to $200,000 through an Activate Provider; pre-Series B and founded in the last 10 years.
- [Cloudflare for Startups](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/) - Credits for Cloudflare's developer platform and network. Offer: $10k, $100k or $350k by tier; founded within the last 10 years, funded up to Series B within the last 12 months, and not previously approved.
- [Confluent for Startups](https://www.confluent.io/startups/) - Managed Apache Kafka and stream processing for early-stage teams. Offer: up to one year of Confluent Cloud in free product credits, amount not published; early-stage startups, with extra benefits through partner venture firms and accelerators.
- [Datadog for Startups](https://www.datadoghq.com/partner/datadog-for-startups/) - Observability and security monitoring for the whole stack. Offer: up to a year of free access and up to $100k in credits; Series A or earlier, new to Datadog, and referred by an official partner.
- [DigitalOcean Startups](https://www.digitalocean.com/hatch) - Cloud infrastructure credits awarded through accelerator and investor partners. Offer: credit level set by your partner, with a $10,000 monthly usage limit; raised $10 million or less, company website and matching business email, no agencies.
- [GitHub for Startups](https://github.com/enterprise/startups) - Credits usable across GitHub Enterprise, Copilot, Advanced Security and Actions. Offer: $10,000 in flexible platform credits for up to 12 months; referred by a partner, funded up to Series B, and not on GitHub Enterprise in the past six months.
- [GitLab for Startups](https://about.gitlab.com/solutions/startups/) - Source control, CI/CD and security on one platform. Offer: Ultimate free for up to 20 seats in year one then 50% off for seed stage, and 50% then 25% off for early stage; pre-seed or seed with up to $5M raised, or Series A or B with up to $20M, new externally funded customers only.
- [Google for Startups Cloud Program](https://cloud.google.com/startup) - Cloud credits usable on Google Cloud, including the Gemini and Gemma models. Offer: up to $200,000 in credits over two years, or up to $350,000 for AI-first startups; pre-seed to Series A with recent equity funding from an institutional investor.
- [HubSpot for Startups](https://www.hubspot.com/startups) - Discounted CRM, marketing and sales tooling. Offer: 90% off year one, 50% year two and 25% year three; pre-seed, seed or Series A with verified funding or an approved partner affiliation.
- [Intercom Early Stage](https://www.intercom.com/early-stage) - Customer messaging and AI support agent for small teams. Offer: 93% off year one, 50% year two and 25% year three; up to $10M raised, fewer than 15 employees, and not a current customer.
- [Microsoft for Startups](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/microsoft-for-startups/overview) - Azure credits, AI services and go-to-market support. Offer: up to $150K in Startup credits unlocked over time; privately held and for-profit, pre-Series C, and under $350,000 in lifetime free Azure credits.
- [MongoDB for Startups](https://www.mongodb.com/solutions/startups) - Atlas database credits and technical advice. Offer: credits across four tiers, amounts not published; under seven years old, Series A or earlier, building a single software product, and not previously accepted.
- [Neon](https://neon.com/startups) - Serverless PostgreSQL credits for new products. Offer: up to $1,000 for self-funded startups with under $1M raised, and up to $200K in combined Neon and Databricks credits for venture-backed startups with at least $1M raised or a recognized accelerator.
- [Notion for Startups](https://www.notion.com/startups) - Free time on the Notion Business plan with Notion AI. Offer: up to 6 months free depending on partner affiliation; non-paying customer, valid work email and fewer than 100 employees.
- [Prisma for Startups](https://www.prisma.io/programs/startups) - Prisma Postgres credits with expert guidance. Offer: up to $10,000 in credits over 12 months for Prisma Postgres, plus 1:1 guidance from Prisma experts and direct Slack support; Pre-seed to Series A, raised venture funding in the last 12 months, founded in the last 5 years; or bootstrapped with 5k+ MRR for 6 months and 2+ full-time team members.
- [Scaleway Startup Program](https://www.scaleway.com/en/startup-program/) - European cloud credits across three stages. Offer: up to €1,000 for Founders, up to €9,000 for Early Stage and up to €36,000 for Growth Stage; less than 5 years old, fewer than 50 employees, and not yet a client.
- [Vercel for Startups](https://vercel.com/startups) - Frontend hosting and platform credits with enterprise features. Offer: up to $30,000 in Flexible Commitment Amount plus Enterprise-tier access for eligible teams.
- [Zendesk for Startups](https://www.zendesk.com/startups/) - Support desk and AI agents for growing teams. Offer: up to 2 years free, baseline 6 months, for up to 50 agents; fewer than 250 employees, Series B or earlier, under 10 years old, and new to Zendesk.

## AI and Compute Credits

- [ACCESS](https://access-ci.org/) - Allocations on United States national advanced computing systems. Offer: use of the systems at no cost to the researcher, allocation tiers set through the allocations process; researchers and educators with or without a supporting grant.
- [Claude for Startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups) - Anthropic program for startups building on Claude. Offer: Claude credits and priority rate limits; founded within the last four years, and credits require equity funding from an institutional investor with no prior Anthropic startup credits.
- [EuroHPC Access Calls](https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/access-our-supercomputers/eurohpc-access-calls_en) - Time on European supercomputers through several access modes. Offer: Benchmark and Development Access are continuously open with resources allocated within two weeks, alongside larger Extreme Scale and AI calls; open to academia, the public sector and industry.
- [Hugging Face Community GPU Grants](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-gpus) - Free GPU upgrades for community demos hosted as Spaces. Offer: a sponsored hardware upgrade for selected Spaces, applied for from the Space hardware settings; no amount published.
- [Lambda Research Grants](https://lambda.ai/research) - GPU cloud credits for AI research projects. Offer: up to $5,000 in cloud credits for qualifying researchers.
- [Modal](https://modal.com/startups) - Serverless GPU compute credits for early-stage companies. Offer: a one-time credit grant, amount not published; seed to Series A with partner venture funding or over $1M raised, with larger packages past Series B.
- [NAIRR Pilot](https://nairrpilot.org/) - Shared national resources for artificial intelligence research and teaching. Offer: compute, data, models and training resources at no cost across research, education, deep partnership and start-up opportunities.
- [NVIDIA Inception](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/) - Free program for AI startups with technical and commercial support. Offer: free cloud credits from NVIDIA and partners plus preferred hardware and software pricing, with no fee and no equity; incorporated, under 10 years old, at least one developer and an active website.
- [OpenAI for Startups](https://openai.com/startups/) - Program for startups building on the OpenAI API. Offer: API credits and rate limit upgrades for accepted startups, mainly through partner venture firms.
- [TPU Research Cloud](https://sites.research.google/trc/about/) - Free Cloud TPU capacity for machine learning research. Offer: access to a cluster of more than 1,000 Cloud TPU devices; researchers who agree to share results publicly and give feedback on the platform.

## Research Grants

- [Amazon Research Awards](https://www.amazon.science/research-awards) - Unrestricted gifts and AWS credits for academic research in Amazon's focus areas. Offer: unrestricted funds plus AWS promotional credits, amounts not published; researchers at academic institutions and non-profits, through periodic calls for proposals.
- [Google Research Programs](https://research.google/programs-and-events/) - Faculty research programs, student fellowships and targeted research awards. Offer: amounts and deadlines are published per program rather than centrally; mostly academic researchers and students.
- [Internet Society Foundation Research Grants](https://www.isocfoundation.org/grant-programme/research-grant-programme/) - Funds research into the Internet's development, security and resilience. Offer: up to $200,000 for independent researchers and up to $500,000 for institutions, through an annual application window.
- [NSF America's Seed Fund](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/) - Non-dilutive seed funding for deep technology companies in the United States. Offer: up to $2 million in seed funding with no equity taken; small businesses working on technology grounded in fundamental science and engineering.

## Government and Regional Programs

- [Australia R&D Tax Incentive](https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/research-and-development-tax-incentive) - Tax offset for companies running eligible research and development in Australia. Offer: an offset on eligible R&D expenditure at rates published by the tax office; companies conducting registered core and supporting R&D activities.
- [EIC Accelerator](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en) - European Commission funding for breakthrough innovations ready to scale. Offer: a lump sum grant below €2.5 million for innovation activities plus €1 million to €10 million in equity investment; startups and SMEs in EU member states and Horizon Europe associated countries.
- [EU Funding and Tenders Portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/home) - Single entry point for Horizon Europe and other European Union calls. Offer: amounts and eligibility are published per call on the portal.
- [Innovate UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/innovate-uk) - United Kingdom innovation agency that runs grant competitions for businesses. Offer: competitions with published funding pots, open to UK registered businesses.
- [Innovative Solutions Canada](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovative-solutions-canada/en) - Funds Canadian small businesses to develop and test prototypes for government challenges. Offer: funding set per challenge, amounts not published centrally; eligible Canadian small businesses.
- [SBIR and STTR](https://www.sbir.gov/) - United States federal seed funding across eleven agencies, with no equity taken. Offer: $50,000 to $275,000 in Phase I and $750,000 to $1.8 million in Phase II; small businesses with fewer than 500 employees.
- [Startup India Seed Fund Scheme](https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in/) - Indian government seed funding routed through approved incubators. Offer: up to 20 lakh rupees as a grant for proof of concept and prototype work, and up to 50 lakh rupees for commercialisation through convertible debentures or debt; DPIIT-recognised startups incorporated no more than two years ago.

## Accelerators and Fellowships

- [Astera Residency](https://astera.org/residency/) - Fully funded residency for building public goods in science and technology. Offer: a salary of $125,000 to $250,000 plus a negotiated project budget of $0 to $1.5M over 12 to 18 months; application cycles run about twice a year.
- [Emergent Ventures](https://www.mercatus.org/emergent-ventures) - Grants and fellowships for people with scalable ideas for social change. Offer: no amount published; applicants aged 13 or over anywhere in the world, with dedicated tracks for India, Africa, the Caribbean and Ukraine.
- [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) - Paid program pairing new open source contributors with mentoring organizations. Offer: stipends from $750 to $6,600 depending on project size and country, paid 45% at the midterm evaluation and 55% at the end.
- [LFX Mentorship](https://lfx.linuxfoundation.org/tools/mentorship/) - Paid remote mentorships on projects hosted by the Linux Foundation. Offer: a stipend funded by the program, with the amount set per mentorship and not published on the site.
- [MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.com/) - Remote program where participants contribute to open source with a mentor. Offer: an educational stipend for fellows, amount not published; applications are open for upcoming start dates.
- [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/) - Paid remote internships in open source for people facing underrepresentation or bias. Offer: a $7,000 total internship stipend; two cohorts a year with applications before each.
- [Thiel Fellowship](https://thielfellowship.org/) - Two-year program for young people who want to build instead of staying in school. Offer: $250,000 over two years.

## Sponsorship Platforms

- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/) - One-off and recurring support with a simple page and no monthly cost. Offer: a 5% transaction fee, so creators keep 95%, with no subscription fee.
- [Drips](https://www.drips.network/) - Streams funding to GitHub repositories and the dependencies underneath them. Offer: no fee published; supports continuous streams, retroactive funding rounds and pull request rewards.
- [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors) - Recurring and one-time sponsorship built into GitHub profiles. Offer: 100% of sponsorships from personal accounts reach the developer, with a fee of up to 6% on sponsorships from organization accounts; open to contributors in over 150 supported regions.
- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/pricing) - Tips, memberships, shop items and commissions on one page. Offer: 0% fee on one-time tips on the free plan and 5% on shop items, memberships and commissions, or 0% on everything with Ko-fi Gold at $12 per month.
- [Liberapay](https://liberapay.com/) - Recurrent donations run as a non-profit with no platform cut. Offer: no cut taken by the platform, only payment processor fees averaging 3% on Stripe and 5% on PayPal; open to anyone funding their work with recurring donations.
- [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/pricing) - Transparent fundraising and spending for groups without a legal entity. Offer: Collectives use the platform for free, funded by a 5% crowdfunding fee or optional platform tips, with paid plans for organizations from $60 per month.
- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/pricing) - Membership platform for recurring paid support from an audience. Offer: 10% of the income earned on the platform, plus payment processing, currency conversion and payout fees.
- [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) - Merchant of record for developers selling subscriptions, licences and digital products. Offer: 5% plus 50 cents per transaction on the free Starter plan, down to 3.4% plus 30 cents on Scale.
- [thanks.dev](https://thanks.dev/) - Routes company donations to the maintainers of the dependencies they use. Offer: no amount published; payouts are based on a sponsor's dependency graph.
- [Tidelift](https://www.sonarsource.com/open-source-maintainers/) - Pays maintainers to keep packages documented, secure and well governed. Offer: income based on customer usage and how important a package is to the software supply chain, amount not published; maintainers of covered packages who complete the lifter tasks.

## Closed Programs

These programs are documented for reference. They are not accepting applications.

- [CZI Essential Open Source Software for Science](https://chanzuckerberg.com/rfa/essential-open-source-software-for-science/) - Funded software maintenance for open source tools used in biomedical research. Offer: awarded $50,000 to $200,000 per year for two years; the last request for applications closed on 17 October 2023.
- [GitHub Accelerator](https://accelerator.github.com/) - Cohort program that paid maintainers to work on their projects full time. Offer: $40,000 per project in non-dilutive funding; the last published cohort ran in 2024.
- [Google Season of Docs](https://developers.google.com/season-of-docs) - Paid technical writers to improve documentation for open source organizations. Offer: grants to participating organizations; the program concluded after six years.
- [Microsoft Accelerating Foundation Models Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/collaboration/accelerating-foundation-models-research/) - Gave academics access to foundation models hosted on Azure. Offer: Azure AI access for selected research proposals; the program has concluded and its pages are archived.
- [Mozilla Open Source Support](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/moss/) - Awarded money to open source projects that Mozilla relied on. Offer: awards across three tracks; on indefinite hiatus and not accepting applications since the 2020 restructuring.
